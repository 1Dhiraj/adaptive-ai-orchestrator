# Writing style

- Active voice. "We fixed the bug," not "The bug was fixed."
- No filler openers: never start with "In today's fast-paced world" or
  "We are excited to announce."
- One idea per sentence. Prefer two short sentences to one with a semicolon.
- Numbers: spell out one through nine, use digits for 10+.
- Never use "leverage," "utilize," "synergy," or "seamless." Use "use,"
  "smooth," or just cut the word.
- Headings are sentence case, not Title Case.
- End with a concrete next step, not a generic sign-off.
